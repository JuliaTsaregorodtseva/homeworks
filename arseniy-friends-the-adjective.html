🤝 Friends

🎯 Attitude & action

⚠️ Careful with these

  • hit it off always has it, and hit is irregular: We hit it off yesterday (not hitted).
  • look up to sb = admire · look down on sb = think you're better than them · look sth up = find it in a dictionary.
  • couldn't care less looks negative but means “I don't care AT ALL”. (Americans also say I could care less with the same meaning.)
  • take sides / take sb's side: Mum always takes my sister's side.
  • FROM my point of view (not on / in my point of view). For an opinion you can also say in my opinion.
  • persuade sb TO do sth (not persuade sb do). Also persuade sb INTO doing sth, persuade sb THAT…. Adjective: persuasive.
  • get a grip on sth = take control of a problem. Get a grip! = calm down. Don't mix it up with get to grips with sth = learn how to deal with something difficult.
  • get your act together: the possessive changes: I need to get my act together. He got his act together.

Step 5 · Grammar

the + adjective = a group of people

Instead of saying rich people or people who are homeless, we can use the + adjective to talk about a whole group in society.

Groups in society

the youngthe oldthe elderlythe richthe poorthe sickthe blindthe deafthe homelessthe unemployedthe injuredthe dead

Nationalities ending in -ch, -sh, -ese, -ss

the Frenchthe Dutchthe Britishthe Spanishthe Irishthe Englishthe Welshthe Japanesethe Chinesethe Swiss

Most others are normal plural nouns: the Russians, the Brazilians, the Poles, the Americans, the Scots.

plural verbThe rich are getting richer.The adjective has no -s (the riches), but the verb is plural.
one persona rich → a rich manFor one person, add a noun: a blind woman, a Japanese student, a Frenchman.
also correctold people = the oldBoth mean the group in general. But the old people = particular old people (e.g. in a photo).

Examples

  • In many cultures the young still look up to the old.
  • The government needs to create more jobs for the unemployed.
  • The English are famous for drinking tea.
  • The Japanese invented instant noodles.
  • Ambulances took the injured to hospital.

Typical mistakes

The poors need help.
The poor need help.
The rich is getting richer.
The rich are getting richer.
She married a French.
She married a Frenchman.
The Russian drink tea.
The Russians drink tea.
The young people today… (in general)
Young people today… / The young today…

⚠️ Careful with these

  • Silent letters: salmon /ˈsæmən/, lamb /læm/, raspberries /ˈrɑːzbəriz/. pear sounds exactly like pair.
  • The animal and the meat: cow → beef, pig → pork, sheep → lamb (young) — but chicken and duck keep the same name.
  • British vs American: aubergine = eggplant, courgette = zucchini, prawns = shrimp, beetroot = beet.
  • roast chicken, roast potatoes (not roasted in everyday British English). baked is for bread, cakes, fish and potatoes in their skin.
  • recipe /ˈresəpi/ = instructions for cooking. A doctor gives you a prescription; a shop gives you a receipt /rɪˈsiːt/.
  • desert /ˈdezət/ island (nobody lives there) ≠ dessert /dɪˈzɜːt/ (the sweet course). Remember: dessert has two s's because you always want seconds!
  • inspire (verb) → inspiration (noun) → inspiring (adjective: the thing) / inspired (adjective: how you feel). The talk was inspiring. I felt inspired.
  • persuade sb TO do sth · predict THAT… · have / make a guess. Predict is about the future; guess is about anything you don't know.
  • cut down ON sth = less of it · cut sth out = none at all. With a pronoun: cut it out.
  • a wrap = a rolled sandwich. Careful: food wrap (or cling film) can also mean the thin plastic you put over food in the fridge.

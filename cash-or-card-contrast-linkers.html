Step 1 · The rules

Three kinds of contrast linker

All seven linkers show contrast, but they don't fit into the sentence in the same way. Look at what comes after each one.

1+ clausealthough · even though · while · whereasAlthough cash is cumbersome, I still use it.I pay by phone, whereas my grandad pays in cash.A clause = subject + verb: cash is, my grandad pays.
2+ noun / -ingdespite · in spite ofDespite the risks, people love paying by phone.In spite of having a card, she paid in cash.Before a clause, add the fact that: despite the fact that cash is cumbersome.
3new sentenceneverthelessCard payments are fast. Nevertheless, they depend on the internet.Formal. After a full stop or a semicolon, followed by a comma. = however.

Surprise or comparison?

surprising resultalthough · even though · despite · in spite of · neverthelessThe second idea is unexpected: Although cash is cumbersome, people still use it. even though = a stronger, more emphatic although.
comparing two thingswhereas · whileTwo different facts side by side: Cash is anonymous, whereas card payments leave a record. while can also mean although (formal): While I see the benefits, I'm not convinced.

🔀 The linker switcher

Choose two ideas, then a linker. See how the sentence has to change, or why the linker doesn't work.

Punctuation

  • Although / Even though / While at the start → comma after the first clause: Although it's cheap, I don't use it. In the middle → usually no comma: I don't use it although it's cheap.
  • whereas in the middle → usually a comma before it: I pay by card, whereas she pays in cash.
  • Nevertheless → full stop (or ;) before it and a comma after it.

Typical mistakes

Despite of the risks…
Despite the risks… / In spite of the risks…
Although it's cheap, but I don't use it.
Although it's cheap, I don't use it.
Despite it is cheap…
Despite being cheap… / Despite the fact that it is cheap…
In spite the rain…
In spite of the rain…
Even it's cheap, I don't use it.
Even though it's cheap, I don't use it.
It's fast, nevertheless it's risky.
It's fast. Nevertheless, it's risky.

🔀 The seven linkers

⚠️ Careful with these

  • cumbersome /ˈkʌmbəsəm/: the b is pronounced, and the stress is on the first syllable.
  • phase out is separable: phase out cash / phase it out (not phase out it). Passive: cash will be phased out.
  • go cashless: The café went cashless (go = become here).
  • to what extent · to some extent · to a large extent: always to … extent.
  • be left behind: Nobody should be left behind. Active: We mustn't leave anyone behind.
  • The downside of something (not downside from).

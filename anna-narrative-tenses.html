Step 1 · The big picture

Three tenses, one story

When we tell a story about the past, we usually need three tenses. Each one has its own job.

past perfectwhat happened beforeI had worked on it for weeks…
past simplethe main events, one after anotherI walked in, said hello and started.
past continuousthe background / in progressThe sun was shining. My boss was talking.

Form

+−?

Interrupted actionI was walking home when it started to rain.long action in progress (continuous) + short action (simple)
Two actions at the same timeWhile I was cooking, he was watching TV.while + continuous

Typical mistakes

I was knowing the answer.
I knew the answer.
She didn't went out.
She didn't go out.
While I watched TV, the phone was ringing.
While I was watching TV, the phone rang.
When I came, the film already started.
When I came, the film had already started.
Step 2

was doing or did?

was / were + -ing = in progress at that moment (the background). Past simple = a finished action, often a short one that interrupts.

Step 3

had done or did?

had + past participle takes us one step further back: it happened before another past action. If actions simply follow each other, use the past simple.

Step 4

All three together

Now mix them. Ask yourself: is it a main event, the background, or something that happened earlier?

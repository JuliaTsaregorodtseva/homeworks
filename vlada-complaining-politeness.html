⚠️ Careful with these

  • compensation is uncountable: We got compensation, not a compensation or compensations.
  • a refund (noun, stress on RE-) · to refund (verb, stress on -FUND).
  • be entitled TO + noun or verb: entitled to a refund, entitled to claim. Not I have a right for a refund.
  • THE runaround, always with the: They gave me the runaround.
  • chase sb up: with a pronoun, it goes in the middle: chase them up, not chase up them.
  • out OF pocket, no article, no possessive: I'm €200 out of pocket, not out of my pocket.
  • MAKE a complaint, not do a complaint. And you escalate a problem, not a person.
  • Register: the runaround, chase up, out of pocket are neutral to informal; escalate and make a formal complaint are the formal end.

Step 5 · Grammar

Politeness through distance

English makes a request polite by moving it away: into the past, into the hypothetical (would), into an indirect question. The request itself doesn't get any weaker. You still ask for exactly what you want.

Try the distance dial

Pick a request, then turn the dial from 1 (an order) to 5 (the most distant).

directdistant

Four patterns, four jobs

Asking for itI was wondering whether…I was wondering whether / if + you could + verbI was wondering whether you could move us today.
Asking what's possibleWould it be possible to…Would it be possible + to do / for sb to do?Would it be possible for someone to call me back?
Saying what to doIt would help if you could…It would help if you could + verbIt would help if you could put that in writing.
Saying noI'd rather you didn't…I'd rather + another person + past simpleI'd rather you didn't put me on hold again.

Past forms for softening

These are past in form but present in meaning. They sound softer than I want or I hope.

  • I was hoping you could refund the difference.
  • I wanted to ask about my booking.
  • We were expecting a sea view, to be honest.

I'd rather: two patterns

Another person does it → past simpleI'd rather you didn't transfer me again.
I do it → bare infinitiveI'd rather not wait until Monday.

Typical mistakes

I was wondering could you help me.
I was wondering whether you could help me.
I'd rather you don't call me at work.
I'd rather you didn't call me at work.
Would it be possible that you send it?
Would it be possible for you to send it?
It would help if you can check.
It would help if you could check.
I'd rather not to wait.
I'd rather not wait.
Please give me another room.
Could you give us another room? (please doesn't fix an order)

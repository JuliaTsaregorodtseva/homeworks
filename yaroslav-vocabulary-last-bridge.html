Step 1 · The words

The words

Read the definition and the pattern, listen 🔊 and say each word out loud. If you need it, the Russian translation is one tap away.

⚠️ Careful with these

  • momentous ≠ momentary. Momentous = hugely important; momentary = lasting only a moment.
    a momentary decision for the country
    a momentous decision for the country
  • be attributed to — always to, and usually passive: The quote is attributed to Wilde. It can also mean “caused by”: His success can be attributed to hard work.
  • wage war / a campaign on or against sb. In the passive: A campaign was waged against corruption.
  • pay homage to sb — not make or give homage. British English pronounces the h: /ˈhɒmɪdʒ/.
  • take pains to do sth — pains is always plural here and often comes with great: She took great pains to be fair.
    He took a pain to explain.
    He took pains to explain.
  • burst – burst – burst: no bursted. She burst into tears. Also: burst out laughing.
  • defy (verb) → defiant (adj) → defiance (noun). peril (noun) → perilous (adj): in peril, at your peril.
  • reassure sb (that)… takes a person directly: She reassured me that… (not reassured to me). Don't confuse it with ensure or insure.
  • throughout = all through a place or time, one word: throughout the country, throughout the war.

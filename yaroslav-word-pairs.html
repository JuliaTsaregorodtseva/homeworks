💬 Word pair idioms

⚠️ Careful with these

  • The order is fixed. Swapping the words sounds wrong to native speakers.
    cons and pros
    pros and cons
    quiet and peace
    peace and quiet
    later or sooner
    sooner or later
    sound and safe
    safe and sound
  • now and again ≠ now or never. Now and again = sometimes; now or never = this is your only chance.
  • sick and tired OF + -ing / noun: I'm sick and tired of waiting.
  • the pros and cons OF sth — usually with weigh up: weigh up the pros and cons of moving.
  • lightning (a flash in the sky) has no e — lightening means making lighter. Both thunder and lightning are uncountable: a flash of lightning, not a lightning.
  • touch and go usually follows it was: It was touch and go whether we'd make it. Before a noun it takes hyphens: a touch-and-go situation.
  • more or less = approximately or almost: more or less finished, more or less the same.
  • by and large usually starts the sentence and is followed by a comma.

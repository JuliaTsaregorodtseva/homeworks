Step 1 · The rules

Different or the same?

Before you choose a linker, ask two questions: Are the ideas opposite or similar? and Am I talking about one thing (two sides of it) or two different things?

1Two sides of ONE thingon the one hand … on the other hand · however · althoughOn the one hand, credit cards are convenient. On the other hand, they make it easy to overspend.Use both halves. Each starts a sentence and has a comma.
2Two DIFFERENT thingswhereas / while · unlike · in contrastI save every month, whereas my brother spends everything.Unlike my brother, I save.whereas + clause · unlike + noun
3Two SIMILAR thingssimilarly / likewise · both … and · like · compared withRent is rising. Similarly, bills are going up.Both cash and cards have advantages.both … and + plural verb

Where do they go?

linkerpositionexample
However, In contrast, Similarly, Likewise, On the other handstart of a new sentence + commaShares can grow fast. However, they're risky.
whereas / whilejoins two clauses (comma before)I rent, whereas she owns a flat.
unlike / like / compared with+ noun or pronounUnlike me, she's never been in debt.
although+ clauseAlthough I earn a lot, I'm always broke.

⚖️ Comparing and contrasting

💰 Personal finance

⚠️ Careful with these

  • debt /det/: the b is silent. be in debt, get into debt.
  • interest /ˈɪntrəst/ = extra money on a loan or savings (also: I'm interested in art).
  • save money ≠ save up for something: I'm saving up for a car.
  • afford: I can't afford to buy it / I can't afford it (not afford buying).
  • spend money on something · splash out on · cut back on.
  • broke (informal adjective) ≠ broken: I'm broke = I have no money.

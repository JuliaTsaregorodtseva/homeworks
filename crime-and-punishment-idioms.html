⚠️ Careful with these

  • Register: almost all of these are informal. Use them in conversation, not in a formal report. The long arm of the law and be brought to book are more neutral and often appear in journalism.
  • Fixed words: pull the wool over sb's eyes (not heads) · do a runner (not make) · brought to book (no article) · on the straight and narrow (fixed order).
  • Possessives change: line his / her / their own pockets · have her hand in the till · pull the wool over everyone's eyes.
  • do time · do a stretch · do a runner: three idioms with do. Length goes before stretch: a five-year stretch (no -s on year).
  • be brought to book is nearly always passive: They must be brought to book.
  • a poacher turned gamekeeper: a poacher hunts animals illegally; a gamekeeper protects them. The reverse, a gamekeeper turned poacher, also exists.

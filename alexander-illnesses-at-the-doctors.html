When did it start? Когда это началось?

The doctor asks:How long have you had it?= When did it start? Как давно это у вас?
It started last night.the easy way · past simple
Since yesterday.since + a point in time · since Monday, since 5 o'clock
For two days.for + a period of time · for a week, for three hours

Useful time words · Полезные слова о времени

  • two days ago«назад» — ago, и оно стоит ПОСЛЕ: two days ago (не ago two days).
  • last night, not in last nightС last, this, yesterday предлог не нужен.
  • once · twice · three timesОдин раз, два раза, три раза: She was sick three times.

⚠️ Careful with these

  • revise = study again before an exam. It is not “repeat” or “check”.
  • pass an exam = be successful. To say you only do the exam: take or do an exam (never make an exam).
  • make / let + sb + verb without to: She made me wait. · be allowed to + verb: We're allowed to leave early.
  • The passive is be allowed to, never be let.
  • go to university (no article) · be at university · go to college (US).
  • The letter u has four sounds: music /juː/ · lunch /ʌ/ · blue /uː/ · put /ʊ/.

Step 5 · Grammar

First conditional & future time clauses

If + present simple, will / won't + verb

We use the first conditional for a possible future situation and its result.

If you work hard, you'll pass.if clause first → comma
The teacher won't be pleased if we're late.if clause second → no comma
Call me if you need help.imperative instead of will
You won't pass unless you revise.unless = if … not (positive verb!)

⏰ The same rule after time words

After when, as soon as, until, before, after we also use the present simple — even though we mean the future.

whenas soon asuntilbeforeafter
If I see Tom, I'll tell him.maybe I'll see him
When I see Tom, I'll tell him.I'm sure I'll see him

Step 5 · Шаг 5

First: adjective or adverb? Сначала: прилагательное или наречие?

Adjective · прилагательноеdescribes a person or a thingMarco is careful. · Lena is a quick painter. · She's a good artist.Какой? — careful, quick, good
Adverb · наречиеdescribes an action (a verb)Marco works carefully. · Lena paints quickly. · She draws well.Как? — carefully, quickly, well

Сравнивать мы будем как человек что-то делает — поэтому нужно наречие. Внимание: от good наречие — well, а fast, hard, early, late не меняются.

How to compare actions Как сравнивать действия

1 · short: + -erfast → faster
hard → harder
early → earlier
late → laterКороткие наречия (как прилагательные) + -er.
2 · -ly: more / less + adverbslowly → more slowly
carefully → more carefully
quickly → more quickly
confidently → less confidentlyНаречия на -ly: more (более) или less (менее) + наречие. НЕ carefullier!
3 · irregularwell → better
badly → worse
far → furtherОсобые формы — их нужно запомнить.
+ than (чем): Lena paints faster than Marco. · Marco works more carefully than Lena.

🔧 The comparison machine Машина сравнений

Tap an adverb and see its comparative form.Нажми на наречие — и увидишь сравнительную форму и правило.

More ways to compare Ещё способы сравнить

as … as = так же…, какAnna paints as quickly as Max.
not as … as = не так…, какMarco doesn't paint as fast as Lena.
much / a lot + comparative = гораздоMarco works much more carefully.
a bit + comparative = немногоShe arrives a bit earlier than me.

🚫 Watch out Частые ошибки

She paints more fast.
She paints faster.
He draws more better.
He draws better.
She works carefullier.
She works more carefully.
as faster as
as fast as
very better
much better
